# Combat characters and growth

## Combat direction

**Direction.** Keep medium lethality with valuable assassinations and practical counterplay. An ambush should reward preparation and reduce the length of a fight, while leaving the victim a readable response. The original v0.1 kit provides a starting experiment: basic melee, positional assassination and dash.

Fog adds opportunities to prepare an attack, but it must not suppress the victim's required warning. Any change to attack damage, recovery, movement or HP needs a corresponding time-to-kill review. Public sight can expose aggression without guaranteeing who is human, who is an AI contender or who is merely a frightened resident.

## Characters and stats

The source conversation's title refers to character design and stats, but the readable user messages do not provide a named roster, approved stat table or confirmed stat formulas. Do not attribute invented characters or numbers to that discussion. Release specifications may propose them explicitly.

Begin with one shared kit until objective play, respawns and vision work together. Later character designs should describe a role, a concealment tradeoff, an active skill, a response available to an opponent and how peaceful growth remains viable. A roster is not required to test the new win condition.

| Stat area | Development policy |
| --- | --- |
| HP and recovery | Keep damage thresholds and recovery windows understandable; avoid unlimited sustain at an objective |
| Damage and attack cadence | Review combined burst and followup time rather than each number alone |
| Movement and dash | Preserve escapes without invalidating map control or wall collision |
| Sight and elevation | Prefer explicit source rules to hidden perception bonuses |
| Cooldowns | State when they begin, whether death resets them and which upgrades change them |
| Profession progress | Separate it from combat level so work can have distinct milestones |
| Gold | Define income, sinks and death loss; prevent duplicated transactions |
| Respawn delay | Calculate from an explicit death-time snapshot and show the resulting countdown |

No persistent account progression or monetization advantage is authorized. In-match level, profession progress and equipment reset at the start of a new match unless a future design decision explicitly changes that.

## Peaceful growth

**Direction.** Nonviolent, lower risk growth is necessary to maintain believable public life. It should award enough XP, gold or materials to support competitive preparation and at least one Heart route. Work should involve choices and travel rather than a stationary button that prints unlimited gold.

The first profession can be a courier loop using civic stations. A later system may add craft specialisation, trade, collection or service roles. This sequence tests whether ordinary activity creates cover before investing in a broad profession tree.

Track peaceful progress independently of kill totals. A match report should be able to show a player completing the peaceful route with zero kills and without relying on a bot destroying the tower network on their behalf.

## Combat rewards and limits

Contender kills may award XP and gold, with anti-feeding rules and victim losses defined. Wild camps offer an alternative combat income. Routine residents should not be profitable farming targets in the new baseline. Their importance is the environment they create.

Tower rewards should motivate contest and acknowledge the contributor who completed the objective. The user suggested powerful upgrades or artifacts for destruction or the last hit. The full game's reward allocation, shared contributions and theft rules remain design questions. A small prototype may use a bounded preparation artifact and a normal XP or gold award before testing a broad artifact pool.

## Upgrades and items

Random upgrade offers and deliberate purchases remain complementary systems. Keep early upgrades capped and avoid damage stacking while the new match rules settle. Changes to maximum HP, movement and cooldowns are easier to interpret when only one or two can be selected.

Vision items, consumable healing and a specialised objective artifact are sufficient for the v0.2 experiment. Secret shops, recipes, rarity tiers, character-specific drops and boss effects remain future content. Design their interactions with death and ownership before adding them.

## Death and retained growth

The emerging direction preserves a player's accumulated growth while imposing time and economic setbacks. Exact retained stats, dropped equipment and recoverable quest items are release decisions. Late respawn time should be a visible consequence of accumulated progression and match phase, not a surprise conversion into permanent elimination.

Objective components require especially clear ownership rules: pickup range, whether they drop on death, whether they can be stolen, what happens on disconnect, whether they survive respawn and when they reset. The peaceful route must have risk, but avoid permanently blocking every route for a player who lost one item early.
