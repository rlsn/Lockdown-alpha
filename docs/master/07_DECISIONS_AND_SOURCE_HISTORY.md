## Proposals and superseded assumptions

Eight slots, level twenty, exact subclass gates, city themes and all numerical coefficients remain proposals. v0.4 uses explicitly labelled smaller defaults. Broad agreement with a conversation does not make every illustrative table a final requirement.

The source assistant claimed the old three-district map arose from Dota's attribute count. That causal history is not established by the project record and is not adopted as fact.

Superseded current assumptions include a fixed hero roster, separate free utility skills, a universal no-RNG rule, 20–30 minute full matches and a permanently fixed three-district map. Old v0.2 and v0.3 values remain historical references only where the new release overrides them.

Remote or noncentral acquisition, advanced suspicion, level twenty, broad factions, procedural geography and a full skillbook economy remain design candidates. v0.4 does not claim to implement them.
