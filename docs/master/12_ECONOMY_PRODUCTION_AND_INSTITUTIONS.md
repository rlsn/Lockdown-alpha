# Economy, work and institutions

## The economic game

Economic play transforms time, information, transport and risk into options. It should support a credible non-kill route while remaining connected to rivals. Professions are activities rather than mandatory second classes. Work/production has concrete requirements and geography; no blanket percentage-income snowball is assumed.

Official supply is a paid reliable source with burn receipts. Regional markets transfer actual stock/cash with stock-dependent quotes. Peers set terms voluntarily. Banks protect committed savings after fees. Workshops transform exact goods and burn fees. These are distinct transaction types and should read distinctly in UI.

## Finite sources and cash

Five full-profile market pools begin with 600 gold each and explicit stocks. Ninety-six civilians contribute 288 initial wallet gold. Rare official stock is shared globally per definition, so visiting another endpoint does not multiply it. Town service access does not create an extra market or rare pool.

The selected full-profile reward mint ceiling is 790N + 1,114, or 20,074 for 24 contenders. With the audited zero-wallet baseline and 3,288 initial circulation, the pre-burn ceiling is 23,362. This is a conservation bound, not a promise that all money enters play or every contender earns a quota. Exact sources and baseline assumptions live in [v0.9 economy](../v09/06_ECONOMY_SOURCES_AND_PROGRESSION.md).

Gold enters through bounded courier/procurement/shortage and encounter rewards. Markets and peer trade transfer it. Official purchases, fees, production expenditure, Charter and death tax burn it. Bank transfers do not mint interest. No resident/guard/ambient farming, automatic gear buyback or respawn endowment is implied.

## Work, discovery and progression

Courier jobs, real Supply/Industrial deliveries and first visits support growth outside combat. One active courier token identifies a contract; another carrier cannot redeem someone else's job. Abandonment is not payout, death does not reset quota and repeated issuance has its own bound.

Witness remains the audited Heave/Parley/profession operation, not a free reward for opening a door. Economic endorsements require distinct deliveries. Crafting, banking, market turnover, repeated surface reactions and movement do not grant passive XP loops.

A zero-kill Charter demonstration must include actual earnings, inputs, Witness, travel, fees and useful counter purchases. An illustrative arithmetic surplus is not proof that a full combat kit is affordable.

The [B92 baseline audit](../v09/B92_ECONOMY_FOUNDATION.md) locates the real Witness
operation: a learned, equipped Heave, Analyze, Parley or Survey action against
its legal service target, with the public score gate and normal action cost.
It grants a single proof receipt, no income or XP. Starting character knowledge
does not imply an extra physical book. A threshold that a fresh preset cannot
meet is a preparation decision to communicate, not permission for the service
to silently grant the missing score or proof.

## Liquidity, geography and confirmation

Regional price differences create transport decisions. A cheap input is useful
only if the contender can afford it, carry it and reach the intended customer
before that customer's cash or stock changes. A nominally rich world can still
have a poor local buyer. Selling gathers a pool's existing money; reaching a
different counter is not a new grant of buying power. Commodity access and
reliable official supply should therefore remain visually distinct.

One confirmation buys or sells one unit at the displayed terms. A competing
transaction can invalidate the quote before commitment. Reject the stale
confirmation without spending, show current terms and let the player choose
again; do not automatically accept a worse price. Repeating an acknowledged
confirmation returns the same result and instance, rather than charging again.
Stock capacity and bag capacity are separate, explainable refusal reasons.

Institution access must not reveal distant personal information. A local quote
can show that counter's authorized price/availability; it grants no rival
wallet, cargo, build or route knowledge. Diagnostic global ledgers remain
outside ordinary player and AI observations.

## Light production and acquisition

Short chains connect Fiber/Ore/Crystal, Toolkits/Cores and specialist equipment. Stations create vulnerable preparation and cargo movement. Work orders can buy missing available official inputs and charge fees, but cannot fabricate rare catalysts or recursively invent components.

Treat crafting and employment as different decisions. A workshop sells a
transformation: the player supplies exact goods, time and a fee, then owns a
new physical output. A procurement counter buys a specified delivery through
a bounded reward source. The same Toolkit can become equipment, a Core input,
market cargo or a Supply delivery; committing it to one path gives up the others.
Neither crafting nor repeated trading grants experience merely for turnover.

A channel reserves selected goods while the character remains exposed. Starting
work must not consume the materials; stopping releases those same instances.
At completion, payment, consumption, output lineage and any shared quota form
one result. A failed or stale confirmation cannot partly charge the player.
Show requirements, duration, fee or reward, and remaining personal/shared
availability before the player chooses. Release of the initiating click is
irrelevant; S and real interruptions stop the order.

Service queues are nonexclusive. Independent contenders can craft at the same
time; another customer's successful work must not invalidate an accepted recipe
whose fee, inputs and eligibility remain valid. Recheck scarce order capacity
at commitment so the final available reward can still have only one winner.

The [B92 physical work review](../v09/B92_WORK.md) connects basic Toolkit/Core
crafting and the Industrial/Supply deliveries to this boundary. It establishes
the first work rewards in the full-map accounting domain. Input placement and
travel in its connected test are staged, so normal discovery, funding and
competition still need an earned journey. Procurement pays for physical goods;
an endorsement alone cannot purchase or activate the Charter without its other
requirements and payment.

Production/consumption uses scheduled bounded opportunities, actual market reserves and stock/capacity checks. A missed opportunity is skipped, without catch-up or deficit funding. Visual travelers do not secretly move economic inventory. A complete physical supply chain is future scope.

An institution's identity outlives its visible cashier, animation and planning
tier. Returning to a shop, replacing an AI seat, reviving a guard or waking a
distant schedule must not recreate its stock, money or reward quota. Consumption
destroys named real goods. Producer expenditure enters a nonspendable sink;
there is no invisible worker purse from which the money can be stolen back.
Skipped work remains skipped after funding returns. This keeps absence and
scarcity consequential without creating a catch-up windfall on wake or reload.

## Property, banking and loss

One physical instance occupies one place: bag/equipment, world/container, market, carried-world binding or installed traversal link. Reservations lock that same instance rather than creating another. Equipped ordinary gear persists through death; exposed cargo, books, consumables and objectives follow their drop categories.

Banking is physical and quiet, shares one account across counters and burns the deposit fee. Treasury Charter payment may explicitly split wallet and bank. There are no assumed loans, insurance or interest. Peer offers do not create protected gold escrow.

## Automatic gathering and finite opportunity

An automatic resource POI removes a needless interaction key while preserving
commitment. Enter its small legal area, become quiet and stationary, and watch
the character channel. The stop decision must be durable: remaining inside after
S cannot immediately start another channel against the player's intention. Make
the restart condition and progress clear. Capacity, interruption and depletion
must retain their real source charges rather than grant a compensating reward.

The [B92 deposit review](../v09/B92_DEPOSITS.md) implements the basic six-second
operation for Crystal, Ore and Fiber. No gold or XP comes from extraction itself;
sale draws a market's finite cash. The learned Survey shortcut remains a distinct
ability operation with its own preparation, score and costs. Both paths must
spend the same source budget. Quantity closure and prepared-state checks do not
establish whether natural travel, competition and gathering feel worthwhile.

## Official access and shared scarcity

A reliable official source exchanges carried gold for a defined item; it is not
a funded regional buyer. Distinguish these roles in the service UI. Limited
stock belongs to the match and definition, not the shop window, body or current
controller. A new endpoint must expose the same remaining quantity rather than
initialize another allowance. Reopening, death, trade and return cannot restock it.

The [B92 supply review](../v09/B92_SUPPLY.md) now implements priced catalog goods,
Circle-I supply, Circle-II training/archive and Circle-III archive purchases.
Each payment, created instance and quota debit share one receipt. Circle-IV
retains its unique-world-source boundary. Purchasing a book does not prove that
its owner can learn or prepare the ability; show the requirements and evaluate
that next step through ordinary progression.

## Banking as a risk decision

Banking changes exposure rather than producing wealth. Choosing when to carry
gold, pay the deposit fee or travel to a quiet counter should matter to the
next purchase and the chance of losing carried money. Small repeated deposits
pay rounding costs repeatedly; explain the total wallet debit, protected credit
and fee before confirmation. A withdrawal restores purchasing power to the
carried wallet and its ordinary risks. Visiting another city must never multiply
the account or reset its limits. The [B92 banking review](../v09/B92_BANK.md)
connects this persistent account to actual counter requests; route safety and
player banking habits still require normal-session observation.

## Accounting and design evaluation

A market is a physical opportunity. Seeing a public location on the map does
not grant remote stock knowledge or permission to transact. The player approaches
the counter, sees its current local terms, and confirms one unit from the actual
bag or pool. The server rechecks access, price, capacity and money together.
If terms change, show the refusal and current quote before another confirmation.
Keep a retry attached to the same receipt so uncertain delivery cannot charge
twice. A full bag should explain the blocked purchase before moving any cash.

The [B92 commodity counters](../v09/B92_SERVICES.md) implement this boundary across
five pools. Their unified ledger preserves the original goods through cache,
bag, ground, market, harvest and consumption. The connected tests stage travel
and later source preparation; finding opportunities and earning a useful build
remain separate design questions. Rare official supply, banks and work need
their own physical access and consequence rules rather than an expanded generic
shop panel.

Global gold end equals start + named mints − burns; transfers cancel. Item end equals start + creation − consumption − explicit cleanup. Recipes retain lineage and finite sources retain receipt identity across actor recreation/death/rejoin.

Measure stockouts, cash exhaustion, net travel margins, wealth concentration, counter affordability and first access timing. Economic depth comes from meaningful choices and competition; repeated unopposed errands are a pacing failure. The public Charter converts economic achievement into a contested opening, never a remote money victory.

Prepared [Survey extraction](../v09/B92_SURVEY.md) now uses the same Crystal charge as basic gathering. Its one-second channel and six-second accepted-start cooldown preserve a real preparation advantage without creating a second resource allowance. Buying or extracting goods grants neither Witness nor passive XP; the Witness operation remains a distinct task at an authored site.
