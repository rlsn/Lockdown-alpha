# Acquisition and build diversity

## Separate progression axes

| System | Axis1 | Axis2 | Tactical alternative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|Equipment|TierI–IV: identity/function/scarcity|Standard/Fine/Masterwork: modest eligible numeric workmanship|Branching recipe/unique active|
|Learned spell|Circle I–IV: complexity/gate|Rank 1–3: personal mastery|Future named manuscript variant|

Higher tier/circle is not automatically optimal in every matchup. A cheap basic dispel can answer expensive silence. Masterwork does not increase attribute points, control duration, charges or prerequisites. Equipment rank boosts affect only authored potency fields, at most one rank capped3.

## Hybrid sources

| Source | Main role |
| --- | --- |
|Official shops/trainers|Reliable equipment components/counters/common books|
|Regional markets/peers|Scarce materials, convenience, resale and player specialization|
|Standard assembly|Predictable incremental combat builds from useful components|
|Specialist workshop|Advanced recipes, deterministic craftsmanship and work orders|
|Ruins/exploration/events|Unique keys, catalysts, rare knowledge and geography|
|Camps/boss/towers|Risky resources, Fragment/book/relic power spikes|
|Theft/death recovery|Existing transferable property, no creation|

Assembly and crafting are complementary. Standard recipes use2–3component definitions and short holds; most ordinary chains have at most two gear-assembly steps. Staff branches choose spell range, dispel or reposition; Halberd+Chain becomes Harpoon, retaining reach and one shared quick-active decision. Component value remains, but some upgrades sacrifice quality, binding flexibility or an offhand. Full work orders can buy available components and charge fees; scarce catalysts still need actual ownership.

Specialized crafting improves quality or produces rare effects at real stations. No random affix lottery, reverse recipe or duplicate-book combining in initial implementation. Heartbreaker needs rare materials and contested forge but no mandatory combat-class lock: its crafter can buy rather than kill for the Fragment. Spells come from starts/trainers/books/ruins/boss and remain learned for the match, not craftable by stacking copies.

Limitless accumulation is constrained by5skills,3quick references,6bindings, one capstone, natural investment, MP/cooldowns and cargo weight. A large backpack is useful logistical capacity, not instantaneous access to every counter. Rare gear offers specific capabilities; cheap counters remain available and useful.
