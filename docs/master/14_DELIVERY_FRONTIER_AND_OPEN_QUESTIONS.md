# Delivery frontier and open questions

Updated 28 October 2026. This chapter orients long-term development; release reports retain exact evidence.

## Reviewed baseline

The native repository starts at ebc19a5, v0.8 B86. Its explicit v08_integration profile has six shared actions, eleven physical bindings, nine presets/traits, 52 abilities, 80 items and 20 recipes. B85 demonstrates all four earned Heart routes on the small integration map; B86 adds native source-aware UI/input and a packaged four-client workload.

B86 reports real limits: one client rendered while three were headless, whole-service timing exceeded its provisional proxy budget, and longer/human/operating acceptance remains open. Preserve those limits. A catalog implementation or earned route demonstration does not establish full-map autonomous competition or 60–90-minute pacing.

## Product questions to answer through play

The [first B91 increment](../v09/B91_IMPLEMENTATION.md) provides native interior
and stair walks plus two-owner door transactions and restricted received views.
Full-map observation uses a separate sparse payload, preserving the old grid
codec. Source revisions drive physical/sight/local-nav changes; repeated snapshots
must not trigger fresh navigation mutations. Rendering a client exposed that
liveness requirement during implementation. Camera cutaways preserve authority
occlusion and present only observed body proxies. The stage remains in progress;
the complete Riverport storage → roof/traversal → grass encounter remains open.

The [storage/cargo checkpoint](../v09/B91_OBJECTS.md) adds finite physical
ownership across ground, bags and containers, plus carried-world props that
suppress hand actions and preserve legal routes. Competing requests and retries
must produce one transfer, not a second reward. Damage/control/life transitions
must preserve the prop and existing contents. This makes ownership, observation
and safe placement shared infrastructure for later portable tools, NPC cargo
and recovery play; it does not establish the later population or economy.

Can a new player find income, prepare a useful toolkit and understand the Heart without external coaching? Does a quiet economic opening remain competitive after buying real defenses? Can a weaker defender identify and execute a legal interruption? Does losing cargo create a new plan or erase all motivation? Do travelers/cities support readable uncertainty without confusing reliable services?

Does the map deliver encounters and route decisions at its selected size? Are the four access paths different in exposure rather than simply one cheapest optimization? Can public openings be exploited by rivals without making purchase irrational? Can multiple builds recover and participate near the deadline?

## Decisions still open

Control diminishing returns, numerical growth, ordinary TTK, counter affordability, route/stock dominance and respawn downtime need measured tuning. Subclasses, spell variants, deeper factions, fully physical caravans, squads and production online services remain candidates. Adding them is not a way to evade unresolved core-loop evidence.

A future squad experiment must explicitly define shared sight, ownership, consent, access and victory. Persistent progression must address fairness across sessions before any power carries over. Do not infer either system from the present solo-contender contract.
