# Delivery frontier and open questions

Updated 30 October 2026. This chapter describes development priorities and the
questions that should guide them. Release reports retain source commits,
commands, counts, failures and immutable evidence.

## What the foundations establish

The reviewed v0.8 B86 baseline contains six shared actions, eleven physical
bindings, nine starting presets/traits, 52 abilities, 80 catalog items and 20
recipes. Its small integration map demonstrates the four earned Heart routes.
Those inherited systems remain regression scope. Its longer-session, hardware
and human limits remain visible; catalogue coverage is not full-map acceptance.

[B90](../v09/B90_COMPLETION.md) supplies deterministic geography and native
movement: terrain bands, ramps, rivers, bridges, settlement identities, service
bindings and alternative approaches. Its measured long empty stretches and clear
in-range sightlines are design diagnostics. They do not predict player traffic,
cover use or encounter frequency in a populated match.

[B91](../v09/B91_COMPLETION.md) connects physical places to source-based actions,
finite goods, observation and synchronized collision/sight/navigation. Native
proof now includes a continuous Riverport journey and reload of its actual world
consequences. The final native matrix and both targets pass. Its source items and builds
are explicitly staged when isolating mechanics; normal acquisition is B92 work.
Permanent stairs remain available without a portable tool. Public fronts cannot
be locked out. Scenery adds obstruction without supplying harvestable rewards.

The user control decision supersedes free contextual lockpick/force and physical
button holds. A future breaching action needs a selected item or prepared ability
with a cost and response. It must not reappear as a generic interaction skill.

B92's [first economic authority increment](../v09/B92_ECONOMY_FOUNDATION.md)
verifies five finite pools, exact stock lineage and funded schedules in native
code. The inherited starter contributes no gold/items, and the real Witness
skill operation has been located. These settle accounting assumptions; they
do not demonstrate legal acquisition or physical service access. The subsequent
[roster increment](../v09/B92_ROSTER.md) supplies actual role-body capacity and
persistent seat takeover/death/return, while routines, security and autonomous
contender progression remain open. Native controller fixtures do not substitute
for remote takeover or human population testing. The [remote service increment](../v09/B92_SERVICES.md)
now checks two real owners, a replacement connection, five physical commodity
counters, actual bag/capacity guards and canonical world/market lineage. Counter
travel and later-city source preparation are fixtures. This establishes a
transaction boundary, while ordinary acquisition pacing, broader multiplayer
capacity and authenticated ownership remain open.

The [banking extension](../v09/B92_BANK.md) connects the same protected account to all five counters, with rounded deposit burn and fee-free withdrawals. Ordinary work, shared rare supply and full recovery remain separate dependencies.

The [official supply extension](../v09/B92_SUPPLY.md) now connects catalog goods and tiered books to selected endpoints with one global Circle-III budget per definition. Two remote owners exhaust one definition in a funded fixture; normal earnings, learning and multiple distinct Circle-III sale sites remain separate acceptance.

The [basic deposit extension](../v09/B92_DEPOSITS.md) now connects nine six-charge POIs to automatic eligible channels and canonical outputs. Three real channels and an explicit all-budget drain validate mechanics/quantity separately. The prepared Survey path is now integrated separately; natural-session gathering competition remains open.

The [prepared Survey extension](../v09/B92_SURVEY.md) now uses ordinary slot/ground confirmation, inherited score/channel/cooldown and the same Crystal budget. Its staged build verifies mechanics, status categories and interruption; its earned progression is established separately by the later Charter route; natural-session pacing remains open.

The [physical work extension](../v09/B92_WORK.md) connects Toolkit/Core crafting
and Industrial/Supply deliveries with exact reservations, timed confirmation,
fees, causal outputs and shared/persistent quotas. Normal discovery/funding,
courier route coverage and broader work interruption/death races remain acceptance work. Witness/registration are connected by the later earned route. Cache inputs and travel in the
connected transaction scenario are explicitly prepared fixtures.

The [courier lifecycle](../v09/B92_COURIER.md) now connects unique sealed parcels,
two remote deliveries, interruption, death/rival recovery and actual replacement
admission. Separate physical-token fixtures close20 payments and40 issuances;
they do not demonstrate20 ordinary journeys. Three authored boards lack in-band
peer routes. Normal route access/teaching, procurement/courier race stress,
earned book learning and Witness/registration are connected by the later route below.

## Earned Witness and registered Charter checkpoint

The [Charter increment](../v09/B92_CHARTER.md) connects all four learned/prepared professions to authored guild Witness targets and Treasury registration to real proof receipts plus one atomic burn of 100 gold from wallet/bank. One zero-wallet, empty-bag remote owner completes six actual courier channels, buys/reads Parley, legally spends earned points and prepares score12, earns Witness, purchases/crafts inputs, gathers Crystal and completes both procurement channels. Negative checks cover score, Silence, Stop, duplicate proof, endpoint, stale wallet, split/balance and repeated purchase. Three alternative profession builds are separate labelled fixtures. Actual disconnect/replacement preserves the same accomplishments.

Travel and protection/quiet setup are staged. This establishes one earned acquisition/preparation/registration chain, leaving natural route teaching/pacing, rival interference, broad work races, useful build diversity and full-session acceptance open. Registration does not implement B93 activation/windows/Heart.

## Delivery sequence

| Layer | Player-facing outcome | Remaining acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B90 spatial world | Places can be reached through ordinary movement and alternative approaches | Complete at its spatial boundary; retain affected-route regressions |
| B91 functional environment | Places can be used, concealed within and changed through authoritative sources | Complete at the functional-environment boundary; retain the source, geometry, privacy and conservation regressions |
| B92 living population and economy | A contender can earn a build and resources through finite institutions, work, exploration and rivalry | Normal acquisition/work pacing, courier route coverage, work race stress, useful build diversity, broader remote admission, routines, witnessed local security and legal AI progression |
| B93 route convergence | Earned preparations produce real capital access, warnings, property risks and a contestable ending | Four full-map routes and exact claim/interruption/deadline behavior |
| B94 complete-session acceptance | The whole match remains readable, consequential and sustainable over its target duration | Normal 60/75/90-minute scenarios, multiplayer/field/density stress, recorded hardware and human pacing observations |

Full personal-account, roster and economy recovery follows those live systems.
B91 checkpoints preserve a settled ownerless environment; they do not silently
promise active-match recovery. Additional spell-to-object interactions follow
authored target modes and material rules. A unit-target spell does not acquire
an arbitrary world-object verb merely because both systems exist.

## Product questions to answer through play

| Question | Useful evidence | Decision it should inform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does the early world offer a legible next step? | First meaningful choice, unprompted explanations, travel/idle share | Service placement, discovery cues and starting opportunities |
| Are preparation and trade-offs understandable? | Actual slot changes, binding/cost mistakes and unused capabilities | Item/ability descriptions, acquisition pacing and comparison UI |
| Does hiding create decisions for both sides? | Observed grass/ledge/door encounters and explanations of lost sight | Cover placement, detector access, reveal cues and approach alternatives |
| Is traversal optional but valuable? | Chosen permanent versus portable routes, shared-link use and interruption | Roof rewards, route time and portable-item opportunity cost |
| Are finite goods contested meaningfully? | Source visits, transfers, scarcity, stock exhaustion and rival interference | Source density, institution budgets and local work |
| Does recovery preserve consequence and participation? | Property retrieval attempts, losses, downtime and abandoned plans | Death/drop communication and return flow |
| Does a public opening change plans? | Warning responses, real travel feasibility and access-owner versus winner | Route windows, warning clarity and capital convergence |
| Does the ending remain explainable? | Winner/permission/interruption receipts and player accounts | Claim feedback, control timing and tie/deadline presentation |

Use legal normal progression for macro conclusions. Prepared-state fixtures
answer whether a mechanic functions, not whether players find or choose it.
A screenshot answers layout questions, not hidden-information or authority
questions. An automated soak does not establish fun or balance.

## Engineering priorities that protect the experience

Keep one identity and one consequence across bags, storage, cargo, death and
recovery. Reject unsupported placement or stale quotes before spending. Keep
client presentation and server geometry consistent, including after changes.
Independent reconstruction of permanent floors must use stable world-space
movement corrections rather than unresolved dynamic bases.

Cull knowledge with geometry and observation, not cosmetic hiding. Public map
geography can remain available; live fogged mutations, wallets, builds, loot and
intentions remain private. AI must use the same allowed facts and real resource
commands. A diagnostic inspector stays outside ordinary client payloads.

Measure performance on stated hardware and settings. Fixed scenery instancing,
local navigation updates and bounded fields are architectural choices, not
measured frame-rate claims. Preserve provisional targets until actual captures
support them. Track path failures, correction/acknowledgment timing, allocation
refusals, source conservation and late combat/control ordering alongside the
human observations.
