# Player journeys, teaching and evaluation

These journeys describe intended decisions, not scripts every player must follow. They connect the chapters into a playable experience and provide repeatable human review tasks.

## First ten minutes: from uncertainty to a plan

A new player selects a teaching preset and sees two starting capabilities, own resources and the persistent action bar. They can identify a public institution, right-click a reachable destination, inspect an observed body without seeing its private sheet, and discover a legal local job or source.

The first transaction should teach cost and commitment. A paid counter, courier delivery or material transform produces understandable inventory/gold feedback. Learning and preparing are separate: the player sees why a known ability is unavailable until placed in a shared action slot or its natural gate is met.

By the end of orientation, the player should name a plausible next investment and explain that victory requires taking Heart. Do not overload the first screen with the entire catalog. Use service context, source tooltips, concise blocked reasons and optional inspection detail.

Observe navigation mistakes, failed context holds, forgotten accessory bindings, apparent invisible blockers and the point at which players lose a goal. An instruction document is not proof of discoverability.

## The quiet merchant who becomes a contender

Start with work and a limited toolkit. Choose routes, buy real inputs, make Supply/Industrial deliveries, earn Witness and decide how much exposed money to bank. Acquire a practical counter or escape tool instead of assuming every spare coin can be saved safely.

Eventually choose between financing equipment, obtaining rare property through trade and purchasing the registered Charter. The Charter activation is a commitment to a public opening; another contender can win it. Forward positioning and prepared disruption matter as much as affordability.

Success evidence is an earned zero-kill path with receipts, travel and meaningful choices. Failure signals include compulsory repetitive jobs, no affordable response to aggression, free Witness substitutes or a purchase that secretly wins remotely.

## The opportunist who learns from observation

Explore a covered approach, see another body use a capability, and infer a limited threat. Prepare detection, mobility, theft or control within the shared budget. Use observation to select an opportunity without knowing human identity, exact HP or hidden cargo.

A rear door, container, roof route and grass escape can support a sequence of approach, risk, acquisition and withdrawal. The world must make legality/cost visible. An unimplemented verb, camera x-ray, hidden-body placement oracle or repeatable source duplication invalidates the encounter.

The player's explanation should be based on witnessed facts: what they saw, what they guessed and what they were willing to risk.

## The stronger fighter and weaker defender

The fighter's investment produces a credible open-duel advantage. They still need sight, range, resources and an access route. The defender can choose a control answer, flank, avoid a strength or disrupt a claim.

At Heart, ordinary damage alone does not cancel. The defender must apply an actual permitted interruption, while the claimant may prepare a specific defense before beginning. Guarded Frost that never applies Stun cannot pretend to interrupt. After the exchange, both players should explain why the claim continued or stopped.

Test readability and agency separately from equal win rates. A defender who could act but could not understand the timing has not received useful counterplay.

## Death and returning with a new plan

Show the retained build, dropped categories, wallet loss and fixed return timer clearly. On return, the player identifies a useful action without repeating every opening purchase. Bank/endorsements and learned knowledge remain understandable accomplishments. Objective cargo loss changes which routes remain feasible.

Ask whether recovering goods is a choice, whether the dropped property is legibly located only when observed, and whether late downtime feels consequential or simply removes participation. Avoid coaching away this feedback.

## The final convergence

A public opening or siege changes the map's strategic situation. A nearby contender evaluates travel and readiness. Someone without access can still enter the precinct and interfere. Dry and Wet approaches offer distinct preparations; neither requires a special movement item.

The ending must be explainable through permissions, channel timing and physical intervention. A single outcome receipt settles exact ties and deadline endpoints. A NoAcquisition result is valid but repeated uneventful expiries are a pacing failure.

## Playtest protocol

Use normal legal progression for macro findings. Label prepared-state fixtures when isolating input, combat or endpoint behavior. Record profile/build, map revision, participants, human versus automated seats, duration, latency and any interventions.

Collect first meaningful choice, first rival observation, travel/idle share, kit changes, source contests, deaths/recovery, first access, failed claims and result. Ask players to explain their plan and perceived alternatives before supplying the intended interpretation.

Separate four conclusions: does the mechanic function, do users understand it, does it create useful decisions, and is its cost/performance sustainable? Each needs different evidence. A 90-minute soak answers none of the human questions by itself.

## Presentation direction

Prioritize silhouette, target category, range/ground footprint and effect timing over visual spectacle. Reuse recognizable cues for Silence/Mute/Root/Stun rather than differentiating only by color. Ambient city activity should make service fronts legible; music/audio intensity may support convergence without disclosing hidden positions. Final art/audio production remains a later pass on a verified interaction language.
