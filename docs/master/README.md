# Project Lockdown — master plan

Revision 7 • 24 October 2026 • Long-term design and release direction

A competitive stealth-to-chaos session RPG/MOBA in a living world. Players begin with a class, then develop six attributes, learned skills, gear and professions. Information, positioning, preparation and high-impact utility can overturn a numerical disadvantage. Combat, commerce, exploration, theft and crafting converge on one Grand Heart acquisition.

Full target: 60–90 minutes, freely connected settlements/wilderness, flexible class starts, one shared combat/utility skill loadout, right-click native navigation, fog and private builds, costly respawn with retained development. Current selected combat defaults: five learned actives, three quick-item references, six equipment bindings, one Circle IV capstone. These are testable defaults, not irreversible balance commitments.

Four access routes lead to the same contested ten-second claim: siege opens globally/permanently; registered Sovereign Charter opens globally/temporarily; three unique keys and constructed Heartbreaker grant temporary personal access. Economic wealth does not win automatically or confer private Charter ownership of the result. A prepared defender can interrupt without winning a DPS duel.
