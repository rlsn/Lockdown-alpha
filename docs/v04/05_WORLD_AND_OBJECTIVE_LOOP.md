# World and objective loop

## A small regional world

Create an authored connected region, approximately 12000 by 8000 cm, with a civic city, two lesser towns and connecting wilderness. Use region IDs and route edges rather than hard-coded three-district indices. The full four-outer-city and capital layout remains future content.

Proposed centres are City at 0 0, WestTown at minus 3600 minus 1000, EastTown at 3600 1400 and WildCrossing at 0 2600. Keep the Heart in the City, the Guild at least 600 cm from its contest area and a preparation station in each settlement. Initial and return spawn points are in the City at least 600 cm from the Heart. Adjust positions for valid geometry and document travel times.

Give each town a public-road route and a covered alternative. Include an outer-to-outer wilderness path that does not require the city centre. Retain trees, ramps, elevated sight and private wards. A single connected level is sufficient; no streaming or procedural generator is required.

Keep at least eight peaceful residents spread across settlements, with role-neutral bodies and routines. Their wallets persist through return. At least one trained NPC uses learned and equipped Patch through the same legal activation path when it needs healing; this tests shared capability without requiring a full AI contender economy.

## Work and exploration

Each contender can complete eight paid courier contracts per match. Accept at the Guild, collect at the assigned town for three stationary held seconds, and return for three held seconds to deliver. Alternate towns from a seeded first choice. Preserve the active destination despite unrelated world events. Keep the 20-second acceptance cooldown after delivery.

Each valid delivery grants 25 gold, 45 character XP and one commission credit once. Death cancels an active job, but paid count and unused credits persist. A new request ID cannot repeat a paid receipt. After eight paid deliveries, disable further paid contracts with a clear explanation.

First visits to WestTown, EastTown and one marked wilderness landmark each award 25 XP once per contender. A visit requires actual traversal into the region trigger by the living unprotected body after protection expires; camera, ward, spawn placement or respawn inspection does not count. No repeat XP from leaving and returning. Eight deliveries plus three visits provide 435 XP before the level-five cap, proving a wholly nonviolent leveling path.

## Wild encounter

One visibly hostile sentinel in WildCrossing provides bounded combat growth. It has 100 HP, 12 melee damage, 96 cm reach, 300 ms windup and 1200 ms start cadence. It acquires visible living unprotected contenders within 500 cm of its origin, leashes at 600 cm, and resets living HP after six seconds without a legal target. It never targets residents or follows into the city.

Return the sentinel 60 seconds after defeat. The living credited finisher receives 45 XP and 25 gold for at most two defeats per contender per match. Credit the greatest direct damage on the lethal authority batch, breaking ties by opaque server ID. A dead finisher receives no reward. Receipt and paid-defeat count prevent duplicate payout across lives. Resident and PvP kills still grant no XP or gold in this profile.

This encounter demonstrates combat scaling and a second XP source. It is not a full camp, boss or loot-system port.

## Surveillance interaction

Keep an actual public watchtower source. It does not shoot passersby. A contender with Analyze equipped and check score at least 14 can channel two seconds at its control within 120 cm to disable the source for 30 seconds. The target has a global 60-second lockout after successful completion. Analyse uses its normal cooldown; cancellation or losing the race gives no effect and no refund of an accepted cooldown.

Publish the offline state as an authorized POI fact, expose the actor for four seconds under sight, remove the public source in the first resulting view, and restore it at the fixed endpoint. It does not change Grand Heart defence flags. This is a real utility-driven world change, not a developer toggle or conquest port.

## Earned Seal and acquisition

At the Guild, a living unprotected character with a Charter Witness, at least four unused commission credits and 100 gold can hold E stationary for four seconds to fabricate one Concord Seal. At most one may be crafted per contender per match; reject if already carrying a Seal. Recheck everything at completion, consume four credits and 100 gold, then create the item atomically. Witness remains as an accomplishment. Interruption costs nothing.

Assembly is a basic objective interaction after learned-skill prerequisites have been earned. It does not cast Analyze or Parley for free and does not create an unslotted reusable crafting ability. No class, level or kill requirement is added. Spending on books or suffering theft can require further work to recover the price.

No ordinary Seal is fixture-seeded. A normal zero-kill contender can complete a starting-skill Charter task, earn four commissions, fabricate the Seal and claim. A thief may recover an earned dropped Seal and use it without the crafter's Witness. Carry capacity is one Seal, separate from four unread books.

Preserve the ten-second Heart channel, 120 cm claim range, 240 cm contender contest, damage and movement interruption, public attempt sensor and one terminal receipt. The ordinary conquest gate stays protected in normal v0.4 because full tower destruction progression is deferred. A carried Seal bypasses it for that contender. UI must say the conquest port is unavailable, rather than imply a working assault route.

Being the only living contender never wins. No acquisition by the 1500 second integration deadline returns No acquisition. Claims whose scheduled completion exceeds that deadline cannot win through a late server sample.

## Duration and test profiles

The normal v0.4 Integration profile caps at 25 minutes and level five. It is intentionally smaller than the full 60–90 minute target. Do not advertise long-session pacing as implemented by stretching this small content loop.

A harness-only 90-minute technical soak can override the deadline and suppress terminal claims to measure lifetime, source, inventory and timer stability. Its grants and overrides are logged and never count as a normal progression or human strategy demonstration. Full pacing, reconnect, population and additional acquisition routes need later human testing.
