# Contracts and the public Charter contest
## Baseline and distinct recognition
Courier remains bounded eight paid jobs, each 25 gold/45 XP; no new legacy commission credits. No craft, gather, regional-sale, bank or peer-trade XP.
Two distinct bound endorsements replace repeated identical certificate farming:
- Industrial endorsement: first valid City Core procurement. Consume one Core for 80 gold/60 XP; repeat Core orders may pay up to three times per contender and 3*N globally but NEVER grant another endorsement.
- Supply endorsement: deliver two Toolkits to WestTown in one commit for 50 gold/30 XP, once per contender and N globally; grant the Supply endorsement.
Both use alive/unprotected, combat quiet six seconds, body sight, 120 cm, a three-second held channel and atomic consume/reward/receipt. Budgets are fixed from the locked roster; no refill on life/rejoin. Legally bought, crafted or recovered goods all qualify. Inputs reserve during a channel, release on cancellation, and cannot serve trade/crafting simultaneously.
These guild commissions are standing opportunities without expiry in the short profile. The shortage bonus order described in markets is finite, shared and expiring; it grants no endorsement. Surplus unpayable goods are never destroyed without explicit consent.

## Purchase
At the City Treasury, acquire one registered Sovereign Charter with BOTH distinct endorsements, retained Charter Witness and 100 gold. The small prototype keeps Witness as its existing learned-skill prerequisite. Endorsements/Witness remain accomplishments rather than consumed currency.
Purchase is an explicit confirmed atomic transaction at the physical service, alive/unprotected, quiet six seconds, sight and range 120 cm. The owner chooses an exact wallet/bank debit split totaling 100; validate current balances and burn the fee once. No interest, remote purchase or protected escrow.
At most one purchase and one successful activation per contender per match. It is a bound registration, not a bag item, tradeable Seal, transferable certificate or automatic win. No new normal Concord Seal is created; legacy Seal items/private access flags are rejected in this profile, including direct requests. Fixtures may retain old behavior only in a labelled legacy test profile.

## Activate and contest
At the City civic terminal, hold F for four seconds to activate the purchased unused registration. Same life/quiet/range/sight checks. Activation completion schedules a public 30-second warning followed by a 60-second global acquisition window. The full-source illustrative 60/120 seconds is compressed for this prototype.
Public message exposes static terminal/window times, not the claimant's private build or an unauthorized body coordinate. Everyone may prepare and EVERY living unprotected contender may attempt the retained ten-second Heart claim during the open window, without owning a Charter. Retain 120 cm range, 240 cm contest, damage/movement interruption, public attempt sensor and one winner.
Opening is an authoritative access flag, not a fake tower deletion. No moving physical gate is required. Protected Grand towers remain intact; full conquest stays unavailable. After closure ordinary access is protected again.
Only one warning/opening may be active globally; another activation is rejected without consuming registration. No extension/overlap/queue. For a claim to win, its scheduled completion must be at or before window close AND match deadline. A sample arriving late cannot grant extra time. Accepted claim interrupted by closure gives no winner.
Activation requires its completion+30+60 <= match deadline, guaranteeing a full window. Purchase requires current time+4+30+60 <= deadline and UI explains later delay can forfeit activation opportunity. Already purchased unused registrations are not refunded.

## Death and persistence
Before activation, death retains registration and endorsements but sets reactivation eligibility to respawn-complete time +30 seconds. Protected state and quiet state still apply. Repeated death recomputes from the newest actual respawn endpoint; no unbounded extension of a displayed respawn wait itself.
Once successfully activated, the warning/window continues even if purchaser dies/disconnects. Registration remains spent; no repeat activation or refund. No one can loot the registration. Physical commercial cargo still drops and can be recovered; bank funds and progress persist.
This chooses the source's registered/recoverable Charter branch and defers lootable/unregistered Charter variants. It explicitly supersedes the older stealable-Seal win route in the normal v0.6 profile.

## Feasible normal path
Three courier jobs earn 75 gold. Produce two Toolkits from stable inputs for 36, fulfill Supply for 50: wallet 89. Produce a Core for 54 using initial City Crystal price 12, fulfill procurement for 80: wallet 115. Buy Charter for 100: 15 remains, before banks/books/coupons/interference. Three jobs+Supply+Core grant 135+30+60=225 XP; three first visits add 75=300, level four, not level five.
This is an initial-state arithmetic feasibility example, not guaranteed quotes or simulated travel. At three successive unchanged-event City Crystal buys, asks are 12,13,14; quotes must be recalculated. NPCs/rivals can change price/supply.
A player may gather Crystal, specialize in market hauling, buy a peer's outputs, or optimize through equipped skills. Two distinct endorsements still require real commissions; trivial repeated jobs alone do not buy victory.
Final demonstrations must include a zero-kill purchaser attempt AND a different contender winning an opened contest without a Charter. Paying controls timing, not outcome.
