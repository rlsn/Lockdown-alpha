# Build plan
## B60 Audit baseline and adopt economy contract
Inspect actual v0.5 delivery, supplies, item policy, commerce, ownership, quota and receipt code. Record differences and preserve engine/movement/UI. Gate: actual baseline and explicit adopted values; no assumed missing implementation.

## B61 Add goods and accounting first
Add five goods, item lineage, institutional cash/stock, finite source/budget identities, protected bank ledger and net cargo-weight validation and gold/item delta records. Gate: replay-safe reset/endowment and aggregate reconciliation before adding economic UI. Depends B60.

## B62 Implement official and regional commerce
Stable paid item creation, real stock/cash transfers, bounded integer prices, quote refresh and native Buy/Sell. Gate: same-market cycle checks, empty/full/cash/stale/race cases and no phantom restock. Depends B61.

## B63 Add production and utility
Finite outcrops, two recipes, reservations/fees/net capacity, Survey extraction and Analyze coupon through equipped skills. Gate: exact consumption/output lineage, interrupted actions no loss, no free XP or unslotted action. Depends B62.

## B64 Add institutional demand and geography
Place services/outcrops along actual roads/covered routes; marked consumer routines and eight bounded supply/demand ticks and one shortage event. Gate: real consumption/empty skip, stock/quote effects, readable public behavior and no hidden-opponent data. Depends B63.

## B65 Connect procurement and contested unlock
Bounded Core and Supply orders, old credit/Seal bypass removed; implement distinct Supply/Industrial endorsements, registered Charter purchase, post-death readiness and a public warning/open/close contest for all. Gate: normal zero-kill economic route with no grants; another contender can win the public opening; one result, budgets persist death/rejoin. Depends B64.

## B66 Integrate economy screens and feedback
Unify catalog/market/workshop/procurement controls and objective hints with v0.5 input/focus/layout/privacy. Gate: actual native screens expose all price/capacity/hold/quote states without dialogue or private-bag leaks. Depends B65; widgets can begin earlier.

## B67 Verify and deliver
Run E01–E48, affected baseline regressions, real remote clients, normal economy scenarios and full ledger reconciliation. Gate: source/assets/data, runnable build where available, completed ledger/report, logs and actual human review or explicit pending evidence. Depends B66.

The release is complete only with real supply/production/market demand and contested acquisition. A timer changing a price label is not an economy; a developer-seeded Seal is not economic progression.
