## Retired preliminary draft
The interrupted pre-attachment draft proposed three identical certificates, exclusive carried Seal access, consumption-only NPC ticks and no bank/weight prototype. Those are replaced, not simultaneous alternatives. Use the complete current documents/data only. No v0.6 ZIP from that preliminary draft was delivered.

The source's optional proximity chat, facility ownership, passive businesses, full markets, equipment families and long-session calibration remain deferred. Official-service disclosure/protection is a narrow institution exception, not a way to label ordinary human bodies.

The prototype price spread includes a next-stock guard: bid cannot reach the ask after adding the sold unit. Enumeration found that a flat 70-percent spread alone allowed four break-even/profitable immediate roundtrips at the selected low-stock targets. This guard is a new validated prototype choice, not a quoted source formula.
