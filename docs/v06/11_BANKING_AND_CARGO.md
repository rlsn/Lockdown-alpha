# Banking cargo and risk
## Physical bank
Use one official bank at City Treasury in this small one-city map; the full vision supports banks in major cities. Open its major panel only alive/unprotected, quiet six seconds, within 120 cm and body sight.
Deposit gross integer amount A: debit wallet A, burn ceil(2*A/100), credit bank A-fee. Reject if A<=fee, insufficient funds, stale balances or overflow. Withdrawal transfers exact A from bank to carried wallet, no withdrawal fee. No remote deposit/withdrawal, loan, interest, insurance or cross-match persistence.
Use explicit confirmation, expected wallet/bank revisions and idempotent request receipt. Incoming theft/other balance change makes stale confirmation fail without partial mutation. No protected gold escrow while the panel is open. Bank credit is protected only after successful deposit. Ordinary purchases/crafting/peer trade use wallet, never bank; only Treasury Charter checkout can explicitly debit both.
A 100 deposit debits 100, banks 98, burns 2. Withdrawal 30 leaves bank 68 and credits wallet 30. Retained death tax and Pickpocket see wallet only. Banks are owner-private, never inferred from a stranger's inspector.

## One inventory with two limits
Keep the delivered 24 slots and add a hard weight capacity, proposed capacity=60+2*(STR-10). Attribute range 8–16 gives 56–72. Weight units: Fiber1, Ore3, Crystal2, Toolkit4, Core6, unread book1, ward charge1, legacy Seal2. Registered Charter and gold have zero item weight because they are not physical inventory instances.
No stacks, cargo-only container, weight-based speed/stealth penalty or new movement formula. This is a deliberate reduction from the source's encumbrance examples. No bag upgrades in v0.6.
Validate BOTH final instance count and final weight for pickup/purchase/recipe output/peer exchange; outgoing items free space and mass. Craft reservations do not double-count weight. Quick slots add no mass/capacity; sorting changes none. UI shows count/weight/limit and precise capacity reason. Every item definition must have a nonnegative weight before play.
Do not allow a build change to reduce capacity below current load. Reject such a preparation draft without spend until load is reduced. No point-allocation exploit can overburden then retain full speed. No respec feature is added.
Carried commodities drop under the retained unique-instance death transaction, outside corpse cleanup. Knowledge/attributes/endorsements/registered Charter and bank persist. No new equipment subsystem is introduced; if the delivered game already has retained equipment, preserve its audited rule and assign weights explicitly.
