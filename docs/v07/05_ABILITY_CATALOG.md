## Multi-step and exceptional semantics

Guard Swap: first cast places a visible owned marker, costs 10 MP, and sets a one-second placement recovery; re-placing destroys the old marker and pays again. Second cast within 20 seconds costs the listed 40 MP, swaps self with that marker, destroys the marker and starts full cooldown. No refund on expiry/death; full cooldown starts if marker is destroyed by an enemy. No two simultaneous markers. Root/grounding, life, full cooldown and endpoint checks apply to both phases; marker placement itself is not a mobility action. Owner may walk away but execution still requires range 450 cm and observable endpoint. Teleport does not path through walls, but endpoints must be walkable and outside protected service collision.

Grand Exchange: two visible ordinary contenders or owned constructs, neither an official service, boss, tower or objective. Both endpoints must fit each capsule and be walkable. Either spell-block charge cancels the entire swap; consume only the first blocking charge in deterministic selected-target order. No half-swap. Root/grounding prevents being swapped. Anchor prevents push/pull only. Either actual swap cancels both units' existing Heart claims. Self-targeting is allowed with one other valid body.

Gate Weave: both ground endpoints must be explored; first within ordinary cast range 650 cm of the caster, pair separation at most 4000 cm. Explored does not require current sight and supplies no dynamic validation feedback about hidden units: use generic placement failure. Both endpoints are staged and created atomically at release, not paid separately. Ground order and visibility do not authorize a hidden-unit target. Owner can have one pair. Ordinary contenders can use either visible endpoint with a fresh one-second held interaction; root, grounding, any hard disable, positive damage, key/fragment/Heartbreaker/relic cargo, or an active Heart claim prevent travel. Destination must be unoccupied, fit capsule, be walkable and stay outside protected service collision. A failed traversal moves nothing. Arrival is vulnerable and has a three-second gate-use lock; traversal grants no invulnerability. Either emitter destroyed closes the pair. Expiry/owner death closes pair. Gates never confer sight through the other endpoint until a legal vision source actually arrives.

Purge Touch: self removes basic negative tags. On another unit, removal of negative tags needs an explicit short-lived aid-consent flag controlled by that recipient; otherwise it removes basic positive effects. In free-for-all, visual friendliness is not consent. Remedy Bind likewise needs consent to remove another's negative tags; self use preapplies one 1.5-second control guard. Aid consent is a simple opt-in capability, no diplomacy or team system. Default off, can authorize one observed contender for 20 seconds, revoked on either life change.

Ledger Mark: debuff is basic-dispellable. Its trail cues are generated only when the marked body occupies cells the caster is currently geometrically allowed to observe. Cues expire four seconds after creation. They may remain as stale ground clues; they never update outside sight or reveal cargo definitions, wallet, persistent identity, or a human label. No full tracking through fog.

Traps trigger against other ordinary contenders/hostile creatures, not owner, decoy or official service. At most two skill traps and two item traps per owner, separately; oldest destroyed before a new one appears. All are attackable when detected, and effect triggers consume the trap. Wards and traps vanish on owner death in v0.7 to avoid respawn ambush stacking. No automatic regeneration of consumed items.
