# Economy integration, death and accounting

## Preserve v0.6 economics

Frozen reference-v06 remains normative for unchanged fixed-price Fiber/Ore/books, three stock-and-cash markets, five goods, two commodity recipes, finite outcrops, eight production/consumption ticks, event600–780, banks, endorsed contracts and public Charter. No extra ticks from lengthening to35 minutes. Gold and items never silently refill on body recreation, life change, GUI reopening or rejoin. Market formulas and next-stock spread guard are unchanged. New gear/books are not eligible for automatic commodity-market buyback.

Every added source has a named budget. For locked N contenders, v0.6 reward mints remain at most540*N+40. New combat adds36 camp rewards*12=432 gold; three towers*40=120; boss90, total642. New maximum ALL reward mints =540*N+682, not including one-time initial endowments. If tower/boss deaths lack an eligible source, actual payout is lower. PvP rewards never mint gold: death tax burns carried gold and physical drops transfer existing property. Initial resident/market gold stays actual audited endowment (example738), not a respawn grant.

Additional new XP: up to3240 global camp XP,450 tower,250 boss; first discovery of each of three ruins grants50 XP once per contender; eligible PvP kills grant50 XP within a300 XP per-contender match budget and180-second attacker/victim-pair cooldown. PvP XP requires an actual opposing eligible contender death caused by the attacker, not a resident/owned decoy, and excludes spawn-protected targets. Persist budget/pair timing across deaths/rejoins; zero new PvP gold. These are bounded integration defaults, not proof of anti-collusion or full balance. Match-private fixture kills do not count as normal evidence.

## Gold identity and sinks

`goldEnd = goldStart + rewardMints - burns` across wallets+banks+market reserves+any other actual spendable purse. Official equipment/books/consumables burn their price and create items. Assembly and quality fees burn. Equipment work orders burn missing official input prices and recipe fees, with source/transform lineage. Existing official commodity purchases, bank deposit fees, producer payments, death tax and Charter fees remain burns. Peer payments, market buys/sales and theft transfer. Buying an item is not also crediting a hidden infinite supplier wallet. No XP or gold reward for assembly/quality.

## Item identity and lineage

For each definition: `end = start + created - consumed - explicit_cleanup` across carried (including equipped), institution-held, cache-held, world drops and actual world sources. Reservations count once, not as another location. Bound equipment is one carried item with binding metadata. Quick references add neither count nor weight. Books teach knowledge through consume receipt; re-reading known books refuses. Recipes consume exact inputs and create output with parent lineage. Quality changes same ID and quality, not definition count. A consumed ward charge becomes one source entity with eventual Expired sink. Mark reset/end-match cleanup separately from in-match sinks.

At reset: three distinct keys, three catalysts and three ruin capstone books are fixed cache endowments, not per-player grants. Boss Fragment/book and tower relics are created once on their respective defeat receipts. Normal merchant output is paid creation; world mobs drop no random gear beyond authored rewards. A rare book cannot also remain in its cache after pickup/read. Heartbreaker activation consumes the physical object and creates a non-item timed lease receipt; key activation creates a lease without consuming keys. Do not count access flags as sellable item instances. Catalyst/Core/material consumption is an item sink even when resulting ordinary equipment retains on death.

## Death transaction and recovery

At death server time D, cancel trade/craft/activation/claim and release all physical reservations first. Apply `ceil(15*wallet/100)` burn to carried wallet only. Bank and residual wallet follow retained v0.6 behavior; do not additionally invent a winner gold bounty. Retain level, allocated points, trained ranks, learned library, ordinary equipped gear, bank, endorsements and registered Charter. Drop all cargo, unread books, unused consumables, unbound gear and designated objectives. Tower Relic drops even while equipped, removing its binding/reference. Keys/Fragment/Heartbreaker always drop. No free new supplies on respawn.

Equipped gear retaining is an explicit new risk rule: spare gear is cargo, combat gear is investment. No disassembly lets players convert retained gear back into protected raw materials. A player may legitimately equip a valuable piece to protect it at the cost of another binding; quiet/loadout commitment and fixed slot limits make this visible opportunity cost. Trades and recipes require unbinding first; no pending trade shields equipped/unbound objectives from mandatory death drop.

Calculate respawn endpoint once: D+min(90,8+2*level+floor(elapsed_seconds/120)) seconds. Show endpoint; never extend an already displayed wait on later pressure. Spawn HP/MP full and protected for three seconds, but all ability/family/instance cooldowns and budgets persist. This respawn resource refill is explicit and costed by downtime, not repeat item/gold mint. Spawn protection blocks attacks, skills, items, pickups, work/commerce and claims; movement to leave spawn is allowed. No invulnerable objective attempt. Corpse cleanup never deletes drop ownership records. Label teleport to spawn as respawn, not gate travel carrying protected cargo.

Public Charter death rules remain exactly as v0.6. Personal key/artifact warning/lease is cancelled on death; no artifact refund. Gate pairs/owned traps/temporary wards/markers end on owner death. Released projectiles may resolve in original lives; no effects attach to a respawned life by persistent ID alone. Ordinary buffs/debuffs/barriers clear; cooldowns do not. Death cannot duplicate a recipe input, finished output and loot drop in the same batch.

## Feasibility and honest balance limits

Original zero-kill source path remains arithmetically75 courier gold−36 Supply inputs+50 reward−54 Core inputs+80 reward−100 Charter=15 gold. It grants300 XP with three first visits, level 4. New counters/gear compete with that money; do not claim the15 gold leftover buys a complete build. Players can take more budgeted work/procurement, trade, gather instead of buying Crystal, or accept risk. Evaluate actual stock quotes/travel and Witness on the delivered game. A zero-kill Charter demo can succeed by timing and defenses without becoming a full fighter. A new template that must buy Parley for Witness must include its actual training/point/time cost; one extra25-gold courier pays40-gold book relative to the15-gold-surplus example, leaving zero under the same unchanged-price assumptions. Further counter purchases need further legal earning. Never silently grant Witness or waive its existing skill score checks.

Full-session income rates, static initial markets, one-of-each capstones, contested unique keys, all-route completion times and rich-player dominance remain playtest questions. No infinite equilibrium or equal win rate is claimed. Tune costs/rewards only with data/prose/assets and upper bounds updated together.
