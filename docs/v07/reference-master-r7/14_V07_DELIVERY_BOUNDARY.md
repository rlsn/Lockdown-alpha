# v0.7 delivery boundary and open design questions

The v0.7 package is a concrete release contract for tactical combat, build development, hybrid acquisition and all four victory routes, integrated with the bounded economy. It contains28 new abilities,40equipment/tactical items,12 new assembly recipes including Heartbreaker, deterministic quality upgrades, tiered book sources,10authored POI nodes and55acceptance cases. Existing utilities/classes/economy remain through audited adapters, not silently replaced.

Selected integration defaults are35 minutes,1–4locked contenders,15 level cap,8–16natural stats,5shared skills,3quick references,6gear bindings, one equipped Circle IV. They differ from the frozen v0.6 regression and must never mix. A cap is capacity for testing, not proof every normal player reaches it. Source data/model validation is supplied; game source/build/native tests are not.

Engineering acceptance covers atomic resources/ownership, filtered perception, cast/effect order, control counters, loadout/cooldown abuse, rare source budgets, gear/death, source/sink reconciliation, all routes/access overlap, claim endpoints, single winner and native independent clients. Normal demonstrations use legal earnings/acquisitions; fixtures isolate boundary cases. Human evidence asks whether users understand effects, can counter stronger builds, choose different loadouts and recover from losses. Unavailable evidence is recorded honestly.

## Still open after the prototype

- Numerical growth, affordability, typical levels/TTK, control-chain frustration and possible DR.
- Whether starting signatures ever remain exclusive; ordinary toolkit stays cross-class.
- Spell variants/subclasses and their budget/identity rules.
- Full-session supply/market/rare-source pacing, route dominance and passive camping.
- Rich AI contenders, law/guards, geography expansion and eventual squads.

Do not solve those by adding unrequested systems to v0.7 or hiding missing required effects. Observe the compressed prototype, adjust synchronized contracts and then decide the next stage.
