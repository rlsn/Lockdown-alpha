# Rivers, unit reactions and bounded fields

## Permanent walkable rivers

All gameplay river polygons have an authored walkable floor connected to banks using the ordinary native navigation graph. River width/depth/art never implies swimming, drowning, bridge dependence, path collapse or impassable ice. River entry adds no base movement penalty. Freeze, Fire, Wind and Shatter never change collision, navmesh connectivity, path links or elevation. Distinguish static deep decorative water outside playable bounds from these navigable river polygons. NPC/player right-click movement must cross thawed and frozen river through the same graph.

Server feet-point/capsule contact with authored water polygon applies Wet immediately on crossing before same-boundary hits, then refreshes every100 ms. Water Jet/Water Flask and Wet cells similarly apply Wet. Leaving all wet sources leaves3000 ms residual (River Waders1500 ms). Frozen river still has underlying water and therefore still refreshes Wet; freezing never grants immunity by drying a river. Strongest residual duration among currently applicable sources; no stacked Wet magnitude. Basic cleanse clears current Wet, but continuous contact refreshes at next server environment boundary. Water contact also removes Burning before that boundary's contact damage. A floor cell Frozen on dry stone is not valid unless a temporary Wet substrate exists.

Frozen cells apply30% movement slow while inside, removed at first authoritative exit/expiry. River Treads changes that source's slow to15%, never immunity to Wet-stun. No slipping, knockdown, momentum or random stun. Basic unit Slow can be dispelled but an intact surface immediately reasserts its own contact slow. Frost Capsule creates surface only and never a direct hit. Leaving/dispelling slow does not remove Wet.

## Wet + direct Frost algorithm

1. A valid hostile unit-target or AoE direct Frost hit reaches this original-life victim after packet reflection/block checks. Snapshot Wet at impact, not at cast start. No eligible Wet means normal damage only; Frost itself does not add Wet.
2. Check direct_frost=true, ordinary hostile victim category, not source/self/owned construct/protected official, and serverTime>=victimWetFrostReadyAt. Only explicit element_hit or element_item_hit may qualify. One reaction attempt per cast+victim-life, regardless of line overlap/ticks/gear.
3. If Wet and eligible, remove current Wet and set victimWideReadyAt=now+6000 ms. This endpoint is contender-bound through death/rejoin and shared by all attackers, skills/items, not per caster. It is not reset by Strong dispel. If already locked, apply normal damage, leave Wet unchanged and do not extend lock.
4. Attempt600 ms Stun. Standard hard-control category immunity or one Control Guard can prevent it; the valid attempt still consumes Wet and sets lock. Spell-blocked packets never reach this step, spend no Wet/guard/lock. If no Stun is applied, an existing Heart claim is not cancelled by the reaction alone.
5. Damage and other authored components resolve in the same atomic combat receipt. Continuous river/rain may reapply Wet at the next100 ms environment boundary, but the six-second lock remains. Other direct unit Slow/Stun effects retain their own rules and are not multiplied by this reaction.

There is no global frozen-unit state, freeze meter, frost-on-frost bonus or passive field-stun loop. A body Wet at0, Frost at1000 ms stuns until1600 and cannot Wet-stun again before7000. A Frost hit at6999 does not extend readiness. At7000 a new direct Frost hit can react if Wet. A guard at1000 consumes charge and prevents Stun but lock still reaches7000. Continuous rain+Frost DoT creates zero repeated attempts because periodic surface ticks are not direct Frost hits.

## Environmental state representation

Static authored substrate: stone, soil, water, flammable socket, metal. Dynamic grid cells are200 cm square aligned to one documented world origin, with at most six condition records Wet/Frozen/Burning/Electrified/Obscured/Overgrown and one Oil overlay. Oil is a temporary fuel substrate, not a seventh condition; it is not a unit debuff. Each record has cellId, state, sourceCastId, sourceOwnerId, sourceLifeId, createdAt, expiresAt, magnitude, revision and damage attribution. Underlying river water persists; a consumed Oil overlay does not regenerate. Fire affects only authored flammable sockets and temporary growth/oil, never arbitrary scenery trees in this release.

Each command carries exact footprint mask from deterministic geometry, not a fluid simulation. Global cap32cells/cast; most new actions explicitly cap16. Snap candidate cell centers inside shape, sort by distance to target disk center or distance along line then perpendicular distance, tie by cellId; take first allowed cells and publish actual mask in caster preview. Lines include their explicit rectangular footprint; cone uses range/angle; radius uses a disk. No hidden bodies influence cell selection. One cast hitting many units does not create one field per unit; Firebolt impact owns one footprint. Existing Smoke Veil/Smoke Flask use Obscured masks under32cap; radius growth may clip at cap, shown before commit. All movement and sight query the same actual mask.

One command can produce at most one transition/cell, with no recursive output processing. Six conditions are not an arbitrary coexistence set: Frozen replaces Burning/Electrified/Overgrown and keeps water/Wet substrate; Fire on Frozen consumes Frozen then makes Wet+Steam, does not also create Burning that batch; Water clears Burning then adds Wet; Lightning requires water/Wet and no Frozen; Nature requires soil and no Burning/Frozen. Oil may overlay dry soil/stone, never river/frozen/burning. Unmatched elemental application does not create an invalid state. Existing Obscured may coexist with any valid surface; multiple clouds union geometry. State overwrite uses newest accepted command source/revision and max existing/new expiry clipped to remaining global lifetime from newest creation; no additive intensity/duration. A replacement never extends beyond per-state lifetime caps in environment.json.

Direct Fire/Water/Lightning hit unit effects: Fire applies Burning3000 ms if victim was dry; if Wet it consumes Wet without applying Burning, while direct damage still resolves. Water applies Wet and removes Burning. Lightning adds no unit shock/stun; Chain Lightning explicitly adds20 raw damage to each Wet chosen victim and does not consume Wet. Frost only the algorithm above. Wind/Nature do not implicitly add unit states; use listed push/root/slow operations. No elemental damage vulnerability multipliers beyond those explicitly listed.

## Caps, expiry and overload

Dynamic cells owner64, match256; static river substrate consumes no dynamic budget. At most512cell-transition evaluations per100 ms boundary, damage every500 ms; once per cell/state/cast/victim-life/tick and strongest same-kind damaging surface only. For a command, reserve all needed new cells and worst-case one transition/cell before cost commit. Capacity exhaustion returns generic field-capacity failure with no cast cost/cooldown; no oldest enemy field deletion. Own same-cell update uses existing allocation. At release revalidate reservation; failure spends nothing and releases reservation. Once a projectile is paid, its reserved field allocation remains held through impact/expiry; blocked/missed projectile releases it. No field that has paid silently vanishes on overload. Expiry always processed; retire empty records before allocation. Environmental overload never delays Heart completion or combat CC to a later timestamp; reject excess not-yet-accepted field commands by server sequence.

Maximum lifetime10s plus stricter state caps: Wet10, Frozen10, Burning6, Electrified4, Obscured6, Overgrown8, Oil8. Trait+item bonuses add once per unique modifier family then clamp. Trait/gear snapshots apply at creation, not retroactively by swapping. Transform outputs have their own stated lifetime and caps; no recursion. Per-owner limits apply to distinct live cells with any owned record; replacement/expiry releases exactly the former owner's allocation. Persistent field source lives for its committed expiry even after caster death; original source life remains attribution, not a target to retarget. This differs from wards/traps/gates/conduits, which vanish on owner death. Delayed source-life fields cannot pay new rewards twice; no self/owner immunity for ambient hazards.

## Damage, consent, visibility and AI

Direct offensive casts exclude source and own constructs, affect other FFA contenders regardless of informal alliances. Environmental contact is neutral: Burning/Electrified damages any ordinary vulnerable body including source; Wet/Frozen slow/Steam affect everyone. Aid consent never exempts a body from environmental hazards. Protected officials/spawn-protected units are immune to harmful statuses/damage but do not clear cells or count as victims; carrier can be Wet while protected, while direct attacks/skills/items remain blocked during protection. No service immunity spreads into surrounding streets.

Burning surface20 raw magical DPS; Electrified18; strongest source of same kind per victim per tick. Burning unit DoT12DPS is a separate status, but if standing in Burning surface use greater source20 rather than32. Different Burning/Electrified hazards sum because distinct states, then ordinary mitigation/barrier. Fixed-point500 ms components; late entry receives only due contact tick, not a full duration total. Environmental damage gives kill credit to the creator of the damaging state, not whoever supplied Wet; no assist mint. Self-kill yields no opposing kill reward; own prior-life field may damage respawned body, attributed to original receipt, cannot hit during protection. XP/budget rules remain inherited.

Steam/Smoke Obscured uses same symmetric occlusion: ray crossing external union boundary is blocked; sources inside a connected cloud component see inside their normal radius subject to static walls, not out across boundary. Separate observed ward on far side may supply its own authorized sight. Detection never penetrates smoke/walls; it can reveal concealment within visible geometry. Gust deletes selected Obscured cells then immediately recomputes authorization; it does not publish hidden source IDs/builds. No Steam damage, friendly smoke immunity or automatic reveal. Overgrown applies15% slow only: it does not create stealth cover, collision, LoS walls or regenerating forests.

AI contenders pay identical MP/slot/cooldowns and can select reactions only from their authorized sensory state. Navigation connectivity unchanged; local movement cost may softly prefer dry/nonhazardous cells using visible-known state, never a hard exclusion. Stop/dash/path recovery must remain usable through slowed river. Civilian routines retain trade/work purpose; no automatic all-NPC combat conversion. At least one near-city river ford and one Heart approach with adjacent dry route must test tactical differences. No river gates access to Charter/shops/Heart without alternatives.
