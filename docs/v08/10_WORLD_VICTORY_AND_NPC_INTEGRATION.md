# World, victory routes and NPC integration

Unchanged normative rules come from the included v0.7 archive's08_WORLD_POIS_AND_NPC_POLICY.md,09_VICTORY_AND_MATCH_STATE.md,10_ECONOMY_DEATH_AND_ACCOUNTING.md and data/world_and_routes.json. Active world data is copied with schema8 in this package; geometry additions below do not change access budgets or global rewards.

Preserve small City/West/East plus wilderness, three camps with36 bounded spawns, three ruins holding unique key/catalyst/capstone bundles, advanced forge, HeartForge, single boss, three guardian towers, shrines and at least two Heart approaches. Four-city/full-session map remains future work. Add navigable water polygons to one ordinary river ford and one Heart approach; each has dry detour, adjacent authored soil/flammable sockets, and no required elemental spell to reach a service/objective. Static water polygons do not become player-owned or endlessly replicating grid cells.

Route authorization OR: global permanent SiegeOpen after all3 towers; public registered Charter after existing Witness/endorsements/100 gold and30s warning/60s open; personal three-key breach30/60 while owner retains all unique keys; consumed physical Heartbreaker breach30/60 tied to same life. All routes lead to the same physical10s Heart claim, not automatic win. Key90s owner+instance reuse locks, activation holds, full deadline feasibility, public route messages, no refund, dropped objectives and access-overlap semantics remain v0.7. No new terrain access route, door destruction shortcut or private Charter flag.

Exact claim cancellation: actual stun (including Wet-Frost), Hex, explicit Interrupt, death, nonzero forced displacement, leaving120 cm/voluntary action, hold/focus loss, authorization loss or match terminal. Damage does not cancel unless lethal. Frozen slow, Wet, Root, Silence, Mute and merely nearby opponents do not cancel. Claimant cannot cast/use an item during claim; external aid/pull may help or interrupt according to actual result. Earliest valid completion wins, exact ties by server start ordinal; due control/deaths resolve before completion at same timestamp. Inclusive finite access endpoint/deadline semantics retained.

River contest examples: claimant in Wet river is vulnerable to one direct Frost interrupt; preemptive Control Guard absorbs that reaction while preserving claim and spends reaction lock. Ice Capsule only slows approach and cannot interrupt a stationary claimant. Fire on icy approach creates Steam, which may obscure both claimant and defender; public objective attempt sensor still reports static location/timing but not body target authorization. Gust clears a local corridor and must recompute fog before a unit-target followup. Lightning surface may pressure HP but never silently cancel capture. Dry detour and timed preparation provide counterplay for characters without elemental spells.

Owned wards/traps/conduits/gates/markers disappear on owner death as inherited (conduit newly included); committed cells persist until their normal bounded expiry, with original-life receipt attribution. Retained ordinary equipped gear stays; all unused consumables, spares, unread books and designated objectives drop. FullHP/MP respawn with3s protection and preserved cooldowns; only corpse cleanup of transient effects, no source/item clone. Wallet tax ceil15%, delay min90s,(8+2*level+floor(elapsed/120)) unchanged. New environmental self-kills give no opposing PvP mint; normal original-source kill attribution still obeys finite reward budgets.

Official stable suppliers are identifiable noncontending service bodies; regional markets dynamic; ordinary residents/nonviolent occupations remain. No required dialogue. Combatant AI evaluates visible Wet, field masks and ordinary target permissions, pays slot/cost rules and does not know hidden build. Civilian AI may avoid known active hazards softly without abandoning work loops. Resident-vs-contender status is not leaked to adversaries. No smart AI is claimed by this document; real AI demos are part of implementation acceptance.
