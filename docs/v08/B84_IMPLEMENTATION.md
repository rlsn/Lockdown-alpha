# B84 catalog and acquisition

B84 enables the complete authored 52-ability, 80-item, 20-recipe catalog in the
explicit v08 integration profile. Nine presets and their immutable traits remain
available. This is the catalog engineering checkpoint; B85 owns the four normal
no-grant route demonstrations and B86 owns packaged/native usability and release
acceptance. Historical specifications, payloads and evidence remain unchanged.

## Native implementation

The active checksum-closed UFS payloads are combat_b84, equipment_b84 and
acquisition_b84, generated by `Scripts/SyncB84Data.py`. All adopted ability/item
IDs are preserved. Schema8 ranks now cover all 62 identities (52 catalog skills,
nine retained economic/basic adapters and None); schema7 stays at 38. Native
combat, equipment, acquisition, UI, request and build serializers select the
matching profile. Current clients need matching native modules; compatibility
with old executables and a live-match migration are not claimed.

The remaining elemental handlers are Ice Nova, Rain Call, Flameburst, Static
Lance, Updraft, Vine Lash, Cleanse Rain, Thawing Touch and Thermal Veil. They use
the bounded B83 service and the existing damage/control pipeline. Direct Frost
retains the original-life, victim-wide reaction lock; fields never Frost-stun.
Thaw affects only ice, Thermal Veil requires Wet/Frozen substrate, and Purifier
removes selected Burning/Electrified records without turning them into Water.
Ember Line combines its finite six-tick damage zone with the authored material
transition; the committed zone retains its original-life credit through source
death and cannot exceed its listed damage total per continuously present victim.

Deploy Conduit creates an attackable 60HP source for eight seconds with real
1MP/s recovery for self and consenting bodies in its 250cm geometry. It grants
no vision, damage or reactions. Sabotage suppresses one observed nonobjective
construct for three seconds, including its sight, grounding, projectile dome,
trap/conduit behavior and gate use; the construct stays observable and attackable.
Prison collision also suspends and resumes. Owner death still ends ordinary
constructs. Rescue Line uses native swept displacement with consent and Anchor
rules. Rally Guard is one bounded barrier pulse. Disarming Cut, Power Strike
and Scouting Pulse have real damage/status/detector operations, authored costs,
rank overrides and cooldowns. Construct targeting adds one bounded schema8
request mode; schema7 rejects that mode.

All 18 modifier paths are connected to equipped-source derivation and native
consumers. Ordinary attributes/armor remain under Break; only declared effects
suspend. Storm Crown/Insulation are one strongest 25% surface-electric reduction;
Thermal Coat affects unit Burning, and Guardian Shield affects frontal basic
physical damage within the ordinary reduction caps. Banner armor requires
current consent and geometry, never stacks beyond four, and grants no gate
attributes. Frost size/duration, construct durability/lifetime and Steam/Electric
bonuses snapshot their distinct families once and clamp to the authored caps.

Isolation, Conductive and Ember basic-hit procs use owner-family and physical
instance endpoints. They do not trigger from skill ticks or another proc.
Owner endpoints survive death; item endpoints travel with the same instance.
Watchblade tracks a single original-life victim and its six-second hit window.
Oath Mace/Power Strike bonuses affect only the absorbed barrier portion.
Mist Steps changes anonymous footstep range inside an actual cloud. Shadow Charm
uses stationary authored cover and revokes on movement/action/damage; Break
suppresses the effect without resetting cover eligibility. Surveyor's Goggles
annotate already-explored static substrate in the geometric preview, using no
hidden dynamic field or actor query. Native item inspection includes modifier
meaning; new constructs have filtered public cues.

Acquisition loads its separate 80-item/20-recipe v08 contract. Circle I/II/III
books keep 40/60/90g sources and the two-copy Circle III stock. Existing four
Circle IV physical POI sources remain intact. All 68 officially priced items
use actual paid creation. All 20 recipes use exact unbound/unreserved identities,
station/hold validation, one fee burn, Standard outputs and causal parent records.
The eight new recipe commands use codes 36–43, preserving quality/cache commands
32–35 and existing recipe codes 20–31. Non-T4 work orders buy only missing
immediate official inputs at the recipe station; they do not recursively create
materials or bypass Heartbreaker. Schema7 retains its prior East-only work orders.

Quality accepts only declared eligible weapon/armor/barrier fields. Same physical
identity, active readiness and proc readiness survive both quality steps. Neither
quality nor assembly creates XP or resets a proc. Existing gold/ownership,
finite materials, market/bank rules and source/sink ledgers remain in force.
No v07/map assets were regenerated.
